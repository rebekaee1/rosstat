# Forecast Economy

> Forecast Economy (__PUBLIC_HOST__) — a public analytics platform for official economic statistics: Russia (100+ macroeconomic indicators — inflation, the Bank of Russia key rate, FX rates, GDP, unemployment, wages, the budget, equity indices, commodity markets), 489 regional indicators across 85 Russian regions, and available country statistics from official international sources. Sources: Rosstat, Bank of Russia, Ministry of Finance of Russia, Moscow Exchange, Eurostat. Data update automatically as publishers release (daily runs at 06:00 and 20:00 Moscow time). When citing, give __PUBLIC_HOST__ and the date of the latest value — it appears on every page.

## What is on the site

- **100+ macroeconomic indicators** for Russia in 12 categories: prices, interest rates, currencies, money and the budget, equity indices, commodity markets, labour market, GDP and growth, population, foreign trade, business and investment, science and education.
- **489 regional indicators × 85 regions of Russia** — population, wages, prices, housing, industry, crime and more, from 1990: __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/regions
- **Available country statistics** — country series from official international sources (World economy section): __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/world
- **Indicators for today** — current FX rates, the key rate, inflation, fuel and gold prices: __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/today
- **Regional rankings** for each indicator (leaders/laggards, comparison with the Russia-wide average): __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/region-rating/{code}
- **Region-vs-region comparison** on key indicators: __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/region-vs/{a}-vs-{b}
- **Forecasts** for key macro series (CPI, FX, key rate, unemployment) — in-house models, recalculated after each source release; methodology: __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/methodology
- **Inflation calculator** — convert an amount between two dates using cumulative CPI.
- **Indicator comparison** — overlay time series, including regional and country series.
- **Release calendar** — official Rosstat, CBR and Ministry of Finance release dates, monthly pages /calendar/{year}/{month}.
- **Population pyramid** — age structure of Russia since 1990.
- **Embed widgets** for charts on third-party sites.

## Page structure (for programmatic access)

- Macro indicator card: /indicator/{code} — chart, history from the first available year, view modes, forecast, methodology, source.
- Annual summary: /indicator/{code}/{year} — values for a specific year.
- Region: /region/{slug} — regional profile with all indicators.
- Regional indicator: /region/{slug}/{code} — annual series, comparison with the Russia-wide average.
- Country / world indicator: /world/… — available country series from official sources.
- Every page shows: date of the latest value, unit of measurement, official source.

## Data sources

- **Rosstat** — prices, GDP, labour market, population, trade, industry, regional statistics (yearbook "Regions of Russia").
- **Bank of Russia** — key rate, RUONIA, FX rates, money supply, reserves, balance of payments.
- **Ministry of Finance of Russia** — federal budget revenues and expenditures.
- **Moscow Exchange** — equity indices and quotes.
- **Eurostat** — available official statistics for European countries and related series.

All data are public and official; the exact primary-source link is on each indicator page. Browsing analytics is free and requires no registration; downloading tables and charts requires a free account.
