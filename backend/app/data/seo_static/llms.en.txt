# Forecast Economy

> Forecast Economy (__PUBLIC_HOST__) — a public analytics platform for official economic statistics by country: charts, tables, rankings, comparisons, and forecasts. Sources are national statistical offices, central banks, Eurostat, and the IMF. Coverage includes the United States, Europe, and other available countries. Data update automatically as publishers release (daily runs at 06:00 and 20:00 Moscow time). When citing, give __PUBLIC_HOST__ and the date of the latest value — it appears on every page.

## Languages

- English: https://forecasteconomy.com
- Russian: https://ru.forecasteconomy.com

## What is on the site

- **Country indicators** — hundreds of thousands of series from official national and international sources: __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/
- **Country rankings** — comparable slices (GDP, population, unemployment, and others): __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/world/rating
- **Indicator comparison** — several series on one chart: __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/compare
- **Country card** — profile and indicator set: __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/united-states, __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/canada, __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/germany, __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/russia
- **Forecasts** — only where the model has passed a quality check; methodology: __PUBLIC_ORIGIN__/methodology
- **Calculators** — inflation, mortgage, compound interest
- **Embed widgets** for charts on third-party sites

## Page structure (for programmatic access)

- Country: /{country} — profile, indicator set, source.
- Country indicator: /{country}/indicator/{code} — chart, history, view modes, methodology, source.
- Annual summary: /{country}/indicator/{code}/{year}
- Cross-country ranking: /world/rating/{concept}
- Every page shows: date of the latest value, unit of measurement, official source.

## Data sources

- **Eurostat** — official statistics for European countries and related series.
- **IMF** — comparable estimates (including GDP at current prices in US dollars).
- **U.S. agencies** — Bureau of Labor Statistics, Bureau of Economic Analysis, Federal Reserve (FRED), U.S. Census Bureau.
- **National statistical offices and central banks** — the primary source of a country series when it is available.

All data are public and official; the exact primary-source link is on each indicator page. Browsing analytics is free and requires no registration; downloading tables and charts requires a free account.
